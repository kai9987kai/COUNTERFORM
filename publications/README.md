COUNTERFORM and predictive disagreement selected identical experiments in all four world–seed instances, and selected/nominal designs matched in all 16 runs. The audit accepted structurally incomplete candidates in two random-acquisition runs. These findings do not establish a general acquisition benefit or physical reliability.
